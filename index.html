About Us

GIQS is a team of experienced programmers who have been creating innovative IT solutions together for several years. We specialize in designing and implementing applications that integrate IT systems - we connect our clients' APIs with external services and build programming interfaces from scratch that enable other companies to seamlessly cooperate with our clients' systems.

Our portfolio includes a dedicated accounting system for automatic contractor settlements and preparation of batch files for SAP. We have extensive experience in integrations with various external platforms: from online payment systems, through official address databases, to advanced marketing tools, mailing systems for collecting reviews, and traditional correspondence generation services.

We have experience in developing websites based on the Episerver CMS system and in implementing projects for companies from the insurance, call center and real estate sectors. Regardless of the industry - we are ready to meet any integration challenge.

Our team consists of top-level specialists: backend programmers specializing in .NET, MSSQL and Oracle database experts, frontend developers, and fullstack developers. Every solution is thoroughly tested by our QA specialists - both manually and with advanced automated tests. We also have experienced Product Owners in our team who can professionally manage your project.

We offer not only programming services, but also comprehensive IT consulting. If you need to design a business process, plan application architecture, or simply get professional advice - we will gladly support you. We believe in an honest approach to the client - sometimes a better solution is to use an existing market product rather than building from scratch. We are not afraid to say it directly and guide the client to the most cost-effective path.

We are fluent in English, which has allowed us to successfully cooperate with clients from the Scandinavian market. We are flexible - both short-term projects and long-term cooperation are equally interesting to us. We follow the latest technological trends, including the development of artificial intelligence, using modern tools to improve our work and achieve better results for clients.
